📋 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I dispute a transaction?
Call the Premier Banking Line or visit any branch within 30 days of the transaction date with your account number and transaction reference.
How do I request a wire transfer?
Contact your dedicated Premier Relationship Manager or call the banking line during business hours. International wires require 2 business days.
How do I update my mailing address?
Address changes must be completed in person at a branch with valid government-issued photo ID.
How do I request a paper statement?
Call the Premier Banking Line or write to the address on your last statement. Paper statements are available for up to 7 years.
